02

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on a device when a website is visited. Similar technologies—including local storage, pixels and software development tools—can also remember information or observe how a service is used. In this policy, “cookies” includes these comparable technologies where relevant.

03

Cookie categories

Strictly necessary

Always available

Technology required to transmit the website, maintain security, remember a privacy choice or provide a feature specifically requested by the visitor. It does not require consent when it is strictly necessary for that purpose.

Analytics

Not in use

Tools that measure visits and interactions so a website owner can understand performance. BLU will not activate these tools before obtaining consent where required.

Preferences and functionality

Not in use

Optional technology that remembers non-essential choices or enables third-party functionality. Where consent is required, it will remain off until chosen.

Marketing and profiling

Not in use

Technology used to follow visitors across services, personalise advertising or create marketing profiles. BLU does not currently use it.

05

Third-party services

Following an external link—such as Instagram, WhatsApp or another provider—takes you to a separate service governed by that provider’s own cookie and privacy settings. BLU does not place their website cookies merely because a normal link appears on this website.

The current website retrieves its typefaces from Google Fonts. This request may transmit technical information such as an IP address to Google, although BLU does not use the request for advertising or analytics. Google Fonts does not receive permission through the optional-cookie controls on this website.

06

If the website changes

Before introducing non-essential analytics, advertising, embedded-media or similar technology, BLU will update this inventory and connect it to the existing consent control, which:

  • keeps optional categories disabled until the visitor makes a choice;
  • offers an equally clear way to accept or reject optional cookies;
  • allows choices by purpose rather than using a single bundled consent;
  • records the choice only for as long as reasonably necessary; and
  • makes withdrawal as easy as giving consent.

The Cookie Preferences link remains available in the footer so visitors can review, change or withdraw their choices. A material change to the categories or providers will require a new choice.

07

Browser controls

Most browsers allow you to inspect, block or delete cookies through their privacy settings. Blocking strictly necessary technology can prevent some requested features from working correctly. Browser controls apply separately to each browser and device.
