Scope of these terms

BLU is registered as a tourism-animation company under RNAAT 36/2026. These terms do not cover package travel, multi-day packages, accommodation reservations, the separate sale of passenger transport or tickets, or any other service reserved to registered travel agencies. Transport may be included only when it is an ancillary part of a BLU activity and is provided in accordance with Portuguese law.

The description, inclusions, exclusions, date, start time, meeting or pickup point and price shown in your written proposal or booking confirmation form part of your contract with BLU. If a specific booking confirmation expressly differs from these terms, the expressly agreed booking condition prevails.

02

Booking and payment

A request or provisional hold is not a confirmed booking. Your booking is confirmed only when BLU has sent written confirmation and received full payment, unless BLU agrees otherwise in writing.

Prices are stated in euros and include the taxes expressly indicated at the time of booking. Only the services listed as included are part of the price. Personal expenses, meals, gratuities, admission charges and other items are excluded unless the confirmation says otherwise.

The person making the booking confirms that they are authorised to book for every guest named and will share these terms and all practical information with them. That person is responsible for the accuracy of the information supplied.

Where a consumer books at a distance, the usual 14-day right of withdrawal does not apply to leisure services that are to be provided on a specific date or during a specific period. The cancellation policy below therefore applies from confirmation, without limiting any other mandatory consumer right.

04

Changes to a confirmed booking

Please request changes in writing as early as possible. Changes are subject to availability and are confirmed only in writing. Any additional price or disclosed supplier cost must be paid before the revised service begins.

A request to move the booking to another date made more than 72 hours before departure will be considered in good faith but cannot be guaranteed. Within 72 hours, a date change is treated as a cancellation and new booking unless BLU agrees otherwise in writing. A reduction in guest numbers does not automatically reduce a private journey’s price.

05

Late arrival and no-show

Please be ready at the confirmed place and time. If you are delayed, contact BLU immediately by telephone or WhatsApp at +351 91 578 4212.

BLU will wait for 15 minutes after the confirmed start time. A journey started late may be shortened so that it finishes at the scheduled time, without a price reduction. If no guest has arrived or contacted BLU by the end of the waiting period, the booking is treated as a no-show and 100% of the price is retained. Waiting longer is at BLU’s discretion and may involve an additional charge agreed with the guest.

How journeys operate

Routes and timings are thoughtfully planned but may need to change because of weather, traffic, closures, strikes, public events, safety concerns or circumstances beyond reasonable control. BLU may make a reasonable change that preserves the character and value of the journey. We will tell you as soon as reasonably possible.

If BLU must make a material change before the journey, you may accept the alternative or receive a refund for the affected BLU service. If BLU cancels and cannot offer a suitable alternative, BLU will refund the amount paid for the cancelled service. BLU is not responsible for independent arrangements or consequential costs, except where liability cannot lawfully be excluded.

We may use qualified guides, drivers, venues and other independent providers. Their own reasonable safety and access rules apply. Any material supplier-specific booking condition will be disclosed before confirmation.

Guest responsibilities

Guests must provide accurate contact details and tell BLU before booking about mobility requirements, allergies, medical considerations or other needs that may affect safe participation. BLU will explain whether the proposed activity can reasonably accommodate them, but guests remain responsible for deciding whether participation is suitable and for following safety instructions.

Children must be accompanied by a responsible adult. The booking contact must confirm children’s ages so that appropriate seating, access and activity requirements can be checked.

Guests are responsible for passports, visas, travel insurance, personal belongings and arriving at the agreed place on time. BLU may refuse or end participation where conduct is unsafe, unlawful, seriously disruptive or abusive. No refund is due in those circumstances, without prejudice to mandatory consumer rights.

10

Questions, complaints and dispute resolution

Please raise any concern during the journey when possible so that we have an opportunity to help. Formal complaints should be sent promptly to hello@blucostatravel.com, with the booking reference and relevant details.

For eligible consumer disputes in the Lisbon Metropolitan Area, consumers may contact the Centro de Arbitragem de Conflitos de Consumo de Lisboa. Information about all recognised Portuguese consumer-dispute entities is available from the Direção-Geral do Consumidor.

Consumers may also submit a complaint through Portugal’s official Electronic Complaints Book.

These terms are governed by Portuguese law. Portuguese courts have jurisdiction, without removing any mandatory right a consumer may have to bring proceedings in another competent court under applicable consumer law.
